# Habit Compass Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Habit Compass Planner ("the App") is a planner for weekly planning and reflection. This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

## Everything you write stays on your device

All the information you enter — your mission, roles, long-term goals, weekly "big rocks", tasks, daily and weekly reflections, and emotional bank account entries — is stored **only on your device**.

- No account is required. There is no sign-up and no sign-in
- The App has no server to send your records to. The developer never collects, views, or transmits them
- Your records are removed from the device when you delete the App

## Calendar access

The App may ask for calendar access (full access) so it can add and update your tasks as calendar events.

- **Purpose:** Used solely to create, update, and delete events that the App itself created
- **About read access:** Full access is required so that an event the App added can later be updated or deleted. The App never reads the contents of your other events or transmits them anywhere
- **Where events go:** Events are written to the iOS Calendar. If they appear in Google Calendar or Outlook, that is because you have configured those as accounts in iOS and iOS syncs them. The App never connects to those services directly
- **Optional:** If you decline, every feature other than calendar sync remains available

## Notifications

The App may ask for notification permission in order to remind you about planning and reflection. All notifications are **local notifications** generated on your device; no external server is involved. Each one can be turned off individually in Settings.

## Face ID / Touch ID (app lock)

Because your records are personal notes, you can optionally lock the App.

- Authentication uses Apple's standard LocalAuthentication framework
- **Your biometric data is managed by the Secure Enclave on your device and is never made available to the App.** The App only receives whether authentication succeeded or failed
- App lock is off by default

## Exporting your data

From Settings you can export your records as Markdown text. The exported data goes only to the destination you choose (mail, Files, etc.). The App never sends it anywhere on its own.

## What the App does not do

The App does **none** of the following:

- Display advertising (it contains no ad SDK)
- Collect usage analytics (it contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK)
- Use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or request tracking permission via App Tracking Transparency
- Share or sell data to third parties

## Third-party services

The App does not embed any third-party SDK or service.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
