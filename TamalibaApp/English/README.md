# TAMALIBA Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

TAMALIBA ("the App") is an app that brings your notes, to-dos, and things to read later together into a single timeline. This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

## Everything you write stays on your device

All the information you enter — the text of your posts, comments, tags, the done state of your to-dos, saved items (bookmarks), reminder dates and times, and so on — is stored **only on your device**.

- No account is required. There is no sign-up and no sign-in
- The App has no server to send your records to. The developer never collects, views, or transmits them
- Every feature works without an internet connection
- Your records are removed from the device when you delete the App

## Apple Watch integration

Notes you enter on Apple Watch are exchanged **only between your Apple Watch and your iPhone** so they can reach the iPhone.

- The transfer uses the standard iOS / watchOS mechanism (WatchConnectivity). This communication stays between your own paired devices and never passes through a server run by the developer or a third party
- When your Apple Watch is not connected to your iPhone, the note is stored on the Apple Watch and sent to the iPhone the next time they connect

## Share sheet (saving from other apps)

You can save article URLs and text to the App from the share sheet in Safari and other apps. What the App receives is simply written to the same storage on your device; it is never transmitted anywhere.

## Notifications

The App may ask for notification permission in order to remind you about individual posts. All notifications are **local notifications** generated on your device; no external server is involved. If you decline, every feature other than reminders remains available.

## Siri, Shortcuts, and widgets

The App supports voice commands such as "Note in TAMALIBA", the Shortcuts app, and widgets on the Home Screen and watch faces. Whatever these features handle stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

Speech recognition is performed by the standard feature Apple provides in the OS. Please refer to Apple's privacy policy for how that is handled.

## Spotlight search

So that you can find past posts from Spotlight search on your iPhone, the contents of your posts are added to the search index on your device. This index stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.

## Exporting your data

From Settings you can export your records as JSON or Markdown. The exported data goes only to the destination you choose (mail, Files, etc.). The App never sends it anywhere on its own.

## iCloud backup

If you have iCloud Backup enabled on your device, the App's data is included in the backup. This is a standard iOS feature, and the backup is managed under your own iCloud account. The developer cannot view its contents.

## What the App does not do

The App does **none** of the following:

- Display advertising (it contains no ad SDK)
- Collect usage analytics (it contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK)
- Use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or request tracking permission via App Tracking Transparency
- Share or sell data to third parties

## Third-party services

The App does not embed any third-party SDK or service.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.
