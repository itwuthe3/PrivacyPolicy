**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the "TideTable" application (hereinafter "the App") created by Takahiro Ito (hereinafter "Service Provider"). This service is intended to be used "as is" without modification.

**Information We Collect**

**The Service Provider does not collect or store any personal information through the App.**

The Service Provider operates no server that receives your information. Tide levels are computed on your device from harmonic constants bundled with the App, so no network requests are made to show tides. The only time the App communicates over the network is when it retrieves weather from the Japan Meteorological Agency website, as described under "Network Access for Weather" below. Accordingly, the Service Provider does not do any of the following:

* Record or transmit usage data (access times, session length, interaction history, etc.)
* Collect device information (IP address, OS version, device identifiers, etc.)
* Send crash logs or analytics data externally
* Serve advertisements or use the advertising identifier (IDFA)

**Network Access for Weather**

When a network connection is available, the App retrieves the weather (conditions, chance of precipitation, wind, temperature, etc.) for the region containing the selected station directly from the Japan Meteorological Agency website (www.jma.go.jp) and displays it.

* The only things sent to retrieve it are **the forecast region number and the AMeDAS observation station number, both of which are published by the Japan Meteorological Agency**. Your current location, the latitude and longitude of the selected station, and any information identifying you or your device are not sent
* The communication takes place directly between your device and the Japan Meteorological Agency website and does not pass through any server operated by the Service Provider. The Service Provider cannot know who retrieved the weather for which region, or when
* As with ordinary web browsing, the Japan Meteorological Agency's server receives information that accompanies the connection, such as the source IP address, the URL and time of each file retrieved, and the app name and type of operating system. The Agency states that it collects such information automatically; its handling is governed by the Agency's own policy (see "個人情報保護について" at https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/kishou/info/coment.html). The App does not store cookies from the Japan Meteorological Agency website
* The retrieved weather is stored on your device so that the most recent data can be shown when you are out of coverage
* If you turn off "Show weather" in Settings, the App makes no network requests at all and deletes the weather stored on your device (on Apple Watch, use the switch at the top of the station list). All tide features remain available with it turned off

**Location Data**

The App uses your device's location only if you grant permission.

* It is used solely to select the tide station nearest to you
* Location data is used only for on-device computation and is **never transmitted externally**. It is not sent when retrieving weather either
* It is not stored. The coordinates are discarded once the nearest station has been determined
* You can use every feature of the App without granting location access by selecting a station manually

**Information Stored on Your Device**

The following information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted externally:

* Selected station and favorite stations
* Notification settings (which tide events, timing, and which days)
* Display settings
* The most recently retrieved weather (the data retrieved from the Japan Meteorological Agency website and the time it was retrieved)

If you have the App installed on both iPhone and Apple Watch, the selected station and favorites are synchronized between your devices using Apple's WatchConnectivity. This communication happens directly between your own devices through Apple's framework and does not pass through any server operated by the Service Provider or any third party.

**Third Party Access**

**The App does not use any third party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services.** The Service Provider shares no data with any third party.

The only third-party site the App communicates with is the Japan Meteorological Agency website, from which it retrieves weather. The information that reaches it is described under "Network Access for Weather".

Payment is processed by Apple; the Service Provider never receives your name, credit card number, or other payment details.

**Data Source**

The harmonic constants used for tide prediction were estimated from the predicted tide levels published by the Japan Meteorological Agency.

* Source: Japan Meteorological Agency Tide Tables https://www.data.jma.go.jp/kaiyou/db/tide/suisan/

Weather is displayed after processing the weather forecasts, regional time-series forecasts, and AMeDAS observations on the Japan Meteorological Agency website, for example by selecting the relevant region and times. The processing was done by the Service Provider; the result was not created by the Japan Meteorological Agency.

* Source: Created by processing the Japan Meteorological Agency website https://www.jma.go.jp/ (weather forecasts, regional time-series forecasts, AMeDAS)

**Disclaimer**

The tide levels shown by the App are astronomical tides only and do not include meteorological effects from atmospheric pressure or wind. Actual water levels may differ by tens of centimeters. **The App is not intended for navigational use.**

The weather shown by the App is what the Japan Meteorological Agency had published at the time it was retrieved, and may not reflect later updates. Forecasts are for the forecast region containing the selected station, not for the station itself. Before heading out, please check the latest forecasts, warnings, and advisories on the Japan Meteorological Agency website.

**Opt-Out Rights**

If you wish to remove the settings and weather stored on your device, simply uninstall the App; all stored information will be deleted.
