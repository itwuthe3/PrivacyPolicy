**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the "TideTable" application (hereinafter "the App") created by Takahiro Ito (hereinafter "Service Provider"). This service is intended to be used "as is" without modification.

**Information We Collect**

**The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

The App does not communicate with any server. Tide levels are computed on your device from harmonic constants bundled with the App, so no external requests are made at any time. Specifically, the App does not do any of the following:

* Record or transmit usage data (access times, session length, interaction history, etc.)
* Collect device information (IP address, OS version, device identifiers, etc.)
* Send crash logs or analytics data externally
* Serve advertisements or use the advertising identifier (IDFA)

**Location Data**

The App uses your device's location only if you grant permission.

* It is used solely to select the tide station nearest to you
* Location data is used only for on-device computation and is **never transmitted externally**
* It is not stored. The coordinates are discarded once the nearest station has been determined
* You can use every feature of the App without granting location access by selecting a station manually

**Information Stored on Your Device**

The following settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted externally:

* Selected station and favorite stations
* Notification settings (which tide events, timing, and which days)
* Display settings

If you have the App installed on both iPhone and Apple Watch, the selected station and favorites are synchronized between your devices using Apple's WatchConnectivity. This communication happens directly between your own devices through Apple's framework and does not pass through any server operated by the Service Provider or any third party.

**Third Party Access**

**The App does not use any third party services (analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or otherwise).** No data is shared with any third party.

**In-App Purchase**

The App offers an optional in-app purchase called "Support the developer". Purchasing it does not change any functionality (all features are available to everyone regardless of purchase). Payment is processed by Apple; the Service Provider never receives your name, credit card number, or other payment details.

**Data Source**

The harmonic constants used for tide prediction were estimated from the predicted tide levels published by the Japan Meteorological Agency.

* Source: Japan Meteorological Agency Tide Tables https://www.data.jma.go.jp/kaiyou/db/tide/suisan/

**Disclaimer**

The tide levels shown by the App are astronomical tides only and do not include meteorological effects from atmospheric pressure or wind. Actual water levels may differ by tens of centimeters. **The App is not intended for navigational use.**

**Children's Privacy**

Because the App collects no personal information at all, it is safe for everyone to use, including children under 13.

**Opt-Out Rights**

The App does not collect any information. If you wish to remove the settings stored on your device, simply uninstall the App; all stored settings will be deleted.
